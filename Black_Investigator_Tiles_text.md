# Black Investigator Tiles text

## (Priority 1) Jasper T.C. Waring

Journalist
Yellow investigator. Once per game, you can move any other figure to a Crossing adjacent to
your figure (ignoring numbered circles). That Investigator cannot move, look for clues, or
execute an arrest this turn.

## (Priority 2) Sg Arthur Ferris

Metropolitan Police
Blue Investigator. Once per game, you can make 1 Boat movement with your figure (from one
crossing to any other Crossing of the same block of water) in addition to your normal movement.
If you do, you cannot look for clues or execute an arrest.

## (Priority 3) Dr Thomas Bond

Metropolitan Police, A Division
Red Investigator. Once per game, you can make 1 Alley movement with your figure (from one
Crossing to any other Crossing of the same block of houses) in addition to your normal
movement. If you do, you cannot look for clues or execute an arrest.

## Smoker

Spitzbergen dog
The yellow Investigator can either move his figure by 1 additional Crossing (but he cannot look
for clues or execute an arrest) or he can continue looking for clues in every adjacent numbered
circle, even if he has already found a clue.
